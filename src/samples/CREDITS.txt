Sample sources bundled with Sable
================================

rock/   Rock Kit drums: Big Rusty Drums by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0 (public domain).
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums
bass/   Electric Bass: Growlybass (Squier Jazz bass) by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0 (public domain).
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.growlybass
wurli/  Electric Piano: Wurlitzer EP200 Electric Piano v1.1 by Greg Sullivan (http://www.sullivang.net/).
        Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
        Changes made: a subset of notes was trimmed, level-matched and converted to Ogg Vorbis.
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos
piano/  Grand Piano: Splendid Grand Piano (Steinway), samples released to the public domain by Akai.
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano

boom/ club/ punch/   Boom, Club and Punch drum kits, and
b808/ bthick/ bsmooth/ btrance/ bhard/ bdnb/   the beat basses:
        from the Sonic Pi sample set (sounds from freesound.org contributors). CC0 1.0 (public domain).
        https://github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi/tree/dev/etc/samples

trumpet/ trombone/ horn/ flute/ violin/ strings/   Brass, flute, solo violin and string section:
        VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios. CC0 1.0 (public domain).
        https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
gacoustic/   Acoustic Guitar: Shinyguitar by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0.
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.shinyguitar
gelectric/   Electric Guitar: Emilyguitar (Epiphone, recorded direct) by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0.
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.emilyguitar

tsax/ organ/ harmonica/ ocarina/ fmpiano/ upright/ harpsi/ vibes/ marimba/ glock/ harp/ perc/
        Tenor sax, pipe organ, harmonica, ocarina, FM electric piano (Yamaha TX81Z), upright piano, harpsichord,
        vibraphone, marimba, glockenspiel, harp and the Percussion Kit:
        Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios. CC0 1.0 (public domain).
        https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
asax/   Alto Sax: Weresax by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0.  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.weresax
bsax/   Baritone Sax: Bear Sax by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0.  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.bear-sax

p_salamander/   Salamander Grand: Salamander Grand Piano v3 (Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm.
        Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
        Changes made: two of the sixteen velocity layers were trimmed, level-matched and converted to Ogg Vorbis.
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
p_steinwayb/   Steinway B Grand: VCSL by Versilian Studios. CC0 1.0.  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

All sets were trimmed, level-matched and converted to Ogg Vorbis for Sable.
